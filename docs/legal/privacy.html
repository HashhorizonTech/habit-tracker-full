Privacy Policy

Tend — last updated 24 September 2026

Tend (“the app”) is a habit, focus and mood tracker made by HashHorizon (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. We keep it short and plain.

What we collect

DataWhy
Account: name, email address, password (stored only as a secure hash)To create your account and let you sign in on any device.
Your content: habits, check-ins, tasks, focus sessions, mood check-ins and tags, badges, XP and app preferences (theme, reminders)To show your progress, back it up and sync it between your devices.
Subscription status: which Plus plan you have, its purchase token and expiry, as provided by Google PlayTo unlock Plus features you paid for.
Technical data: IP address and basic request logs on our serverTo keep the service secure and fix problems. Logs are kept for a limited time.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos or advertising ID, and there are no ads or third-party trackers in the app.

Where your data lives

Your data is stored on your phone and on our servers so nothing is lost if you change or reset your phone. Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Mood check-ins and habits can be personal — only you can see them; our staff access account data only when needed to support you or keep the service running.

Payments

Subscriptions and purchases are processed by Google Play. We never see or store your card or bank details. Google’s privacy policy applies to payment data: policies.google.com/privacy.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with service providers that run the service for us (cloud hosting, Google Play for billing), under contracts that require them to protect it, or when the law requires us to.

Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete your account and all its data at any time from inside the app (You → Settings) or by following the steps at https://hashhorizon.in/app/tend/delete-account. Deleted data is removed from our live systems immediately and from backups within 30 days.

Your rights

You can access, correct, export (CSV/JSON, from the app) or delete your data, and withdraw consent by deleting your account. If you are in India these rights are provided under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023; if you are elsewhere, your local law may give you similar rights. Contact us at support@hashhorizon.in and we will respond within 30 days.

Children

Tend is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
