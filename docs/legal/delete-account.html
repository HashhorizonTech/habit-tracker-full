Delete your Tend account

Tend by HashHorizon

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Tend and sign in.
  2. Go to You → Settings → Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Your account and all its data are deleted immediately.

Without the app

Email support@hashhorizon.in from the email address you use for Tend, with the subject “Delete my Tend account”. We’ll confirm and delete it within 7 days.

What is deleted

Your account (name, email, password), and all your habits, check-ins, tasks, focus sessions, mood check-ins, badges and preferences. Data is removed from our live systems immediately and from backups within 30 days.

What is kept

Nothing that identifies you. Google Play keeps its own records of purchases as required by law. Deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play subscription — cancel it in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
